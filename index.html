01 / What is DOJO

A shared evaluation layer for clinical AI.

Distributed Open Justice Oversight is an open, community-driven platform for adversarial evaluation and remediation of health AI systems.

It helps researchers, clinicians, and evaluation teams examine the full system—not only a model score—before and during clinical use.

01Open

Shared tools, challenge sets, and evidence that can be reviewed and improved.

02Local-first

Designed to work near the data and within institutional security requirements.

03Community-driven

Clinical, technical, and lived expertise shape what gets tested.

02 / Architecture

From data to care, evaluated as one system.

Three connected planes make failures visible at the point where they enter the system.

01

Data plane

Checks cohort integrity, missingness, documentation inequity, leakage, and spurious signals.

02

Model plane

Challenges robustness, calibration, rare cases, shifted inputs, unsafe confidence, and abstention.

03

Clinical workflow

Tests interoperability, infrastructure, human-AI interaction, and readiness in context.

03 / Team

Built across disciplines.

DOJO brings together the perspectives needed to test the system around a model, not only the model itself.

Clinical research

Technical practice

ML practitioners and engineers

Build evaluators, reproducible pipelines, and evidence that can be inspected.

Public accountability

Community reviewers

Bring local context, lived experience, and challenge to what counts as readiness.
